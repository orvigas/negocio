## Brand & Style
The design system channels the warmth, flavor, and lively community atmosphere of an authentic Jalisco birriería. Rooted in traditional Mexican culinary craftsmanship, it embodies a tactile, high-contrast, and deeply appetizing aesthetic. Every visual cue stimulates the senses—drawing direct inspiration from slow-simmered chiles, deep red adobo consomé, golden toasted corn tortillas, and handcrafted artisanal signage.

The target audience ranges from passionate food lovers and neighborhood regulars to diners seeking hearty comfort food through digital ordering, menus, and brand experiences. Interfaces evoke genuine hospitality, festive vibrancy, and culinary pride. Rather than relying on cold modern minimalism or generic dark interfaces, this design system celebrates rich, deep adobo surfaces, warm golden amber accents, and creamy highlights that deliver mouth-watering contrast.

## Colors
The color hierarchy is explicitly grounded in the rich crimson and amber spectrum of slow-cooked birria adobo. The default interface mode is warm and saturated, anchored on deep chile and terracotta undertones rather than neutral grays or absolute blacks.

- **Primary (`#c23829`)**: The core adobo red, used for primary actions, badges, prominent brand highlights, and interactive states. Supported by darker stew tones (`#991b1b` and `#7f1d1d`) for depth and containment.
- **Secondary (`#f59e0b`)**: Warm toasted amber and maize gold (`#d97706` to `#f59e0b`), evoking crispy quesabirria edges, melting cheese, and handcrafted gold-leaf lettering. Used for ratings, promotional chips, and secondary calls-to-action.
- **Tertiary (`#155e75`)**: Deep Jalisco agave teal, directly inspired by the decorative filigree borders in traditional birriería crests. Acts as an accent for fresh balance, tag counters, and special status cues.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: Backgrounds transition across warm terracotta and charred chile hues (`#2a0808` to `#450a0a`), topped with rich adobo surface tiers (`#5c0d0d` and `#701414`).
- **Typography & High Contrast Elements**: All body, headline, and label text utilizes warm crema tones (`#fffbeb`, `#fef3c7`) and bright ivory (`#ffffff`), guaranteeing maximum legibility, appetite appeal, and visual punch over deep adobo backgrounds.

## Typography
Typographic expression pairs the hearty, artisanal character of **Epilogue** for headings with the clean, highly legible warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and navigational UI.

- **Headlines & Display (Epilogue)**: Heavy weights (700 and 800) give titles the structural presence of handcrafted rotulismo signage and vintage tavern cartouches. Tight line-heights deliver confident, bold hero banners and section dividers.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Balanced rounded terminals echo friendly Mexican hospitality while preserving effortless scannability across ingredients, order modifiers, prices, and status indicators.
- **Letter Spacing**: Micro-labels and price tags leverage slightly expanded tracking for clear recognition over vibrant chili-red background layers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid 12-column grid system on desktop (`>1024px`), transforming into an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column compact flow on mobile screens (`<768px`).

- **Outer Margins**: Desktop layouts breathe with a generous `2rem` margin to comfortably host celebratory background motifs and warm borders. Mobile experiences compress outer margins to `1rem` to maximize horizontal touch targets and visual real estate for food photography.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Spacing scales in consistent multiples of 4px and 8px. Item groups in menu grids maintain `space-md` gaps, while section containers and festive card clusters breathe through `space-xl` separation.
- **Container Strategy**: Main product catalogs and interactive ordering trays utilize sticky bottom anchors on mobile, retaining uninterrupted visual access to carts and subtotal actions.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this design system is established through fiery tonal layering, warm ambient radiance, and tactile golden borders rather than cold neutral shadows:

- **Surface Layering**: The foundational canvas rests in deep dried-guajillo burgundy (`#2a0808`). Elevated surfaces (cards, bottom sheets, menus) rise into warm braising adobo tiers (`#4a0e0e` to `#611313`).
- **Warm Glow Shadows**: Elevated floating elements use amber- and chili-tinted drop shadows (e.g., `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(185, 28, 28, 0.45), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`). This simulates warm under-counter lighting and steam-table radiance.
- **Artisanal Framing Outlines**: Cards and actionable surfaces are framed with subtle 1px to 1.5px borders in translucent golden maize (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`) or vibrant crimson (`rgba(194, 56, 41, 0.4)`), reflecting the ornamented filigree typical of traditional Mexican rotulismo.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` provides a friendly, organic visual rhythm that complements the round curves of handmade tortillas, cazuelas, and hand-painted shop emblems.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard inputs, quantity buttons, and compact item tiles.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px)**: Dish showcase cards, modals, and checkout bottom sheets.
- **Extra Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px)**: Feature heroes, promotional banners, and floating menu panels.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`)**: Category chips, tag badges, status indicators, and primary floating call-to-action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid fill in adobo red (`#c23829`) or vibrant amber-gold (`#f59e0b`), text set in bold ivory (`#ffffff`) or deep charred burgundy (`#2a0808` for golden buttons). Features a subtle inner top highlight (`1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`) and warm crimson ambient shadow. Hover states intensify warmth with slight upward translation and expanded amber glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Translucent background (`rgba(255, 251, 235, 0.08)`) with a 1.5px warm maize border (`#f59e0b`) and crema text (`#fffbeb`).
- **Interactive State**: Active press produces a tactile scale down (`0.98`) to mimic physical feedback.

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Dish Cards**: Stacked or horizontal layout featuring rich adobo surface background (`#4d0f0f`), wrapped with a 1px border in toasted gold (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`). Dish titles are set in crema (`#fef3c7`), with prices highlighted in golden amber (`#f59e0b`).
- **Combo & Feature Cards**: Enhanced by background subtle gradient transitioning from adobo red (`#7f1d1d`) to terracotta (`#b45309`), bordered by double-stroke decorative trim.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Chips**: Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`). Unselected state features dark chili surface (`#3b0a0a`) with warm border and crema text. Selected state switches to saturated golden yellow (`#f59e0b`) with dark chocolate typography (`#2a0808`) and bold weight.
- **Spicy & Specialty Badges**: Compact pill badges in fiery scarlet (`#b91c1c`) featuring small pepper icons and micro-label typography in crisp ivory.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Steppers**: Dark adobo containers (`#3b0a0a`) with 1.5px border (`rgba(254, 243, 199, 0.2)`). Focus ring produces an intense golden glow (`#f59e0b`). Placeholder text uses soft masa cream (`rgba(255, 251, 235, 0.5)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular and rounded-square toggles filled with dark adobo tone, switching on selection to vibrant golden amber (`#f59e0b`) with a deep crimson checkmark or inner dot.

### Specialized Birriería Components
- **Consomé & Toppings Selector**: Horizontal stepper row featuring quick-add badges for chopped cilantro, diced onion, lime wedges, and salsa macha.
- **Order Floating Bar**: Fixed bottom tray rendered in rich adobo crimson with rounded top corners (`rounded-t-2xl`), ambient warm top-glow shadow, real-time item counter in agave teal (`#155e75`), and high-contrast amber checkout trigger.
