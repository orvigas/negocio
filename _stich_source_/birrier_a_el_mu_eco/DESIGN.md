## Brand & Style

This design system channels the sensory richness, communal energy, and artisanal heritage of authentic Mexican birria traditions. The aesthetic translates the slow-simmered depth of chiles secos, toasted spices, rich consomé, and scorched comal textures into a vibrant, contemporary digital vernacular.

The design movement combines **High-Contrast Folkloric Realism** with **Modern Rotulista Craft**:
- **Personality:** Bold, generous, unapologetic, rustic yet impeccably structured. It balances the warmth of a bustling neighborhood birriería with the precision of a modern culinary flagship.
- **Target Audience:** Dedicated birria lovers, nocturnal diners, culinary explorers, and digital-first patrons seeking authentic beef birria, tacos dorados, quesabirrias, and rich consomé.
- **Emotional Response:** Immediate appetite arousal, festive familiarity, and trust in deep artisanal seasoning and slow-cooked quality.

## Colors

The palette draws its energy directly from the birria cooking vat: slow-rendered guajillo and ancho chiles, caramelized tallow, roasted spices, and the bright golden glimmer of bubbling consomé.

- **Primary (`#b91c1c` - Chile Rojo Caldo):** The anchor of appetite and cultural urgency. Used for primary CTAs, active status markers, category badges, and vibrant framing.
- **Secondary (`#c2410c` - Adobo Tostado):** A fiery, scorched red-orange reflecting comal searing. Applied to promotional callouts, secondary actions, and micro-accents.
- **Tertiary (`#eab308` - Maíz & Grasa Dorada):** Highlighting crisp tortilla edges, cilantro-onion-lime garnish accents, ratings, and checkout confirmations.
- **Neutral Dark Base (`#1c1412` - Fondo Brasa Ahumada):** A blackened earthen charcoal with a warm clay undertone that prevents sterile digital darkness and allows fiery reds and warm cremas to radiate.
- **Surface Offsets:**
  - `surface-container`: `#261a17` (rich roasted clay)
  - `surface-container-high`: `#33221e` (spiced card background)
  - `surface-container-highest`: `#432c27` (elevated states and sheets)
  - `on-surface`: `#fbf5ea` (crema fresca warm white for crisp legibility)
  - `on-surface-variant`: `#d6c3b3` (toasted masa cream for secondary descriptions)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy honors traditional Mexican sign-painting (*rotulismo*) through **Epilogue**, utilizing its punchy vertical cuts, heavy ink density, and geometric chiseled terminals for high-energy display heads and body storytelling.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Epilogue ExtraBold and Black. In headline treatments, uppercase locks with tight negative tracking emulate hand-lettered taquería boards.
- **Body Text:** Epilogue at regular weight provides tactile warmth, rhythm, and comfortable reading across ingredient breakdowns and culinary lore.
- **Labels, Pricing, & Metadata:** Entrusted to **Space Grotesk** in bold and medium weights, formatted with uppercase letter spacing to create high-contrast modern price tags, combo callouts, and order tracker stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an 8-point rhythmic grid, designed to balance dense menu ordering flows with generous food photography and artisanal storytelling.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Focuses on single-column food cards, horizontal scrolling chips for taco selections, and sticky bottom order drawers.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 2rem` and `gutter-md: 1.5rem`. Menu items reflow into 2-column masonry or symmetrical splits.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column responsive layout capped at a maximum width of 1280px with `margin-lg: 3rem` and `gutter-lg: 2rem`. Provides persistent order cart sidebars and multi-column platter builders.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Standard spacing tokens scale tightly from compact ingredients (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) to structural section demarcations (`space-xl`), ensuring content never feels dry or detached from the feast.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids cold, artificial drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Ember Layering** and **Scorched Comal Outlines**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep earthen charcoal (`#1c1412`) with subtle low-opacity radial glows of simmered crimson (`rgba(185, 28, 28, 0.08)`) concentrated in background header zones.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Solid `#261a17` bordered by a 1px crisp outline in `#432c27` (warm terracotta rim).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Cards & Dropdowns):** Solid `#33221e` anchored by an ambient warm shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(12, 8, 7, 0.7), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(185, 28, 28, 0.15)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Sticky Cart Drawers):** Solid `#3d2621` with high-elevation rim lighting: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` accompanied by a top edge ember border of `1.5px solid #c2410c`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary balances modern ergonomic touch surfaces with traditional hand-crafted cuts:

- **Standard Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used on menu cards, input fields, condiment selectors, and informational tiles. Reflects sturdy ceramic plates and comal blocks.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to major promo panels, featured dish showcases, and bottom sheets.
- **Extra Large / Pill:** Reserved strictly for pricing pill tags, spicy indicator badges, and circular counter steppers (+ / - tacos).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Pedir Ahora" / "Agregar a la Orden"):** Background solid Chile Rojo (`#b91c1c`), text in Crema Fresca (`#fbf5ea`), typography `label-lg` uppercase. Hover state deepens to `#991b1b` with a subtle golden halo border (`#eab308`). Active state scales slightly (98%).
- **Secondary ("Ver Menú" / "Personalizar"):** Transparent background with a 1.5px solid border in toasted adobo (`#c2410c`), text in `#fbf5ea`. Hover transitions fill to `rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.15)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure text in `#eab308` with a borderless hover effect highlighting in `#33221e`.

### Chips & Category Filters
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `space-xs space-md`.
- Unselected: Background `#261a17`, border 1px solid `#432c27`, text `#d6c3b3`.
- Selected: Background Chile Rojo (`#b91c1c`), border 1px solid `#eab308`, text `#fbf5ea`, accompanied by a small flame or lime pip icon.

### Cards (Menu Items & Specialties)
- Background `#261a17`, 1px border `#33221e`, roundedness `0.5rem`.
- Features full-bleed 16:9 or 1:1 image of the dish with high saturation, a top-right floating price stamp (Maíz Gold pill `#eab308` with black text), followed by headline in Epilogue bold and itemized condiments (cebolla picada, cilantro, salsa de chile de árbol, limón).

### Inputs & Quantity Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Dark earthenware surface (`#1c1412`), border 1.5px `#432c27`, text `#fbf5ea`, placeholder in `#8c736a`. Focus state triggers a vibrant 1.5px border in `#c2410c` with zero outer fuzzy halo.
- **Quantity Stepper:** Pill enclosure with `-` and `+` touch points in `#b91c1c` around a bold center numerical count in `#fbf5ea`.

### Checkboxes & Radios (Protein, Salsa, & Tortilla Customization)
- Custom square (`0.25rem` radius) or circular controls in `#33221e` bordered with `#c2410c`. Checked state fills solid `#b91c1c` with a high-contrast white checkmark icon.

### Consomé Heat Level Indicator (Specialty Component)
- Custom metric badge displaying 1-3 dried chiles denoting piquancy: Mild (Chiltepín sutil), Medio (Adobo clásico), Bravo (Habanero & Árbol toreado).
